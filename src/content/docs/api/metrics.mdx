Evaluate translation quality independently of the translation endpoints—useful for QA pipelines or scoring translations produced outside Algebras. Algebras offers four complementary evaluation methods across three focused pages, each measuring different aspects of translation excellence.

## Choose Your Evaluation Method

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fluency Evaluation" icon="sparkles">
    Three methods to assess how naturally a translation reads in the target language: dimension-based scoring, batch processing, and MQM-style error evaluation.
    
    **Best for:** Naturalness, readability, language quality across five dimensions (idiomatic, collocational, discourse, pragmatic, calque).
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="GEMBA Quality Scoring" icon="bars">
    Scores meaning preservation and grammatical correctness on a 0-100 scale, optionally calibrated with a human reference.
    
    **Best for:** Overall translation quality assessment when you need a single 0-100 score.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Multi-Metric Evaluation" icon="rocket">
    Run up to five standard MT quality metrics in one call: GEMBA-MQM, GEMBA-ESA, chrF, BLEU, and COMET—each with its own scale and methodology.
    
    **Best for:** Comprehensive QA when you need multiple perspectives, research, or specific metric requirements.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Quick Comparison

| Method | Endpoint | Single Item | Batch | Reference Needed | Scale | Best For |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|------------------|-------|----------|
| **Fluency** | `/fluency-score` | ✓ | — | no | 1-10 / dimensions | Readability assessment |
| **Fluency Batch** | `/fluency-batch` | — | ✓ (20 max) | no | 1-10 / dimensions | Bulk fluency checks |
| **Fluency (MQM)** | `/fluency-mqm` | ✓ | — | no | 0-100 | Detailed error analysis |
| **GEMBA** | `/gemba` | ✓ | — | optional | 0-100 | Single quality score |
| **Multi-Metric** | `/metrics` | ✓ | — | optional | varies | Multiple perspectives |

## Integration with Glossaries

For complete QA coverage, pair these metrics with the [Term Counter](/api/glossaries/term-counter/):
- **Metrics endpoints** → Evaluate translation **quality** (naturalness, fluency, accuracy)
- **Term Counter** → Validate translation **consistency** with your glossary

Together they ensure translations are both high-quality AND follow your terminology standards.

## Getting Started

1. **Start simple:** Try [Fluency Evaluation](/api/metrics/fluency/) with `/fluency-score` for a quick quality check
2. **Scale up:** Use [Fluency Batch](/api/metrics/fluency/) with `/fluency-batch` for bulk processing
3. **Deep analysis:** Explore [Multi-Metric Evaluation](/api/metrics/multi-metric/) with `/metrics` for research or detailed QA
4. **Calibrated scoring:** Use [GEMBA Quality Scoring](/api/metrics/gemba/) with a reference translation for the most accurate 0-100 score

All endpoints charge a flat 10 words per call (or per metric in multi-metric mode).
